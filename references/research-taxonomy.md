# Research taxonomy for these skills

## General umbrella categories
- technology adoption research
- developer ecosystem research
- programming language adoption analysis
- platform/tooling trend analysis
- enterprise technology strategy research
- technical due diligence
- architecture and platform selection research

## Agentic-specific categories
- agent framework ecosystem analysis
- AI application stack research
- language/runtime selection research for agentic systems
- orchestration/runtime trade-off analysis for AI agents
- provider SDK and interoperability landscape analysis
- enterprise readiness analysis for agentic products

## Key recurring evaluation dimensions
- adoption and momentum
- ecosystem maturity
- official vendor support
- talent availability
- production readiness
- enterprise fit
- integration burden
- governance / security / observability
- portability and lock-in risk
- consequences of not adopting
