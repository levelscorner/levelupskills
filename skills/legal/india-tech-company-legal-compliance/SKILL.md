# India Tech Company Legal Compliance

## Overview

Use this skill to produce practical, founder-readable legal/compliance guidance for Indian tech companies.

## Core Principle

Legal/compliance guidance should help the founder build a durable document and process foundation while clearly separating hard obligations from items that require licensed professional confirmation.

## Important framing

- Provide research and implementation guidance, not formal legal advice.
- Prioritize official Indian sources first: MCA, India Code, MeitY, CERT-In, CBIC/GST, RBI, SEBI, EPFO/ESIC, Startup India.
- Distinguish confirmed obligations from items needing lawyer/CA/CS confirmation.
- Flag regulated-risk product features clearly.

## Default scope

Unless told otherwise, assume:
- Indian private limited or similar startup entity
- software / SaaS / AI / app business
- non-bank / non-regulated by default unless features trigger review

## Always cover

- entity and governance documents
- tax / filing support documents
- employment / contractor / IP documents
- website / SaaS legal pages
- privacy / security / incident materials
- founder action list

## Workflow

1. Identify entity and product type.
2. Separate hard requirements from applicability checks.
3. Identify regulated-feature triggers.
4. Recommend document stack and operating hygiene.
5. End with needs-counsel-confirmation items.

## Output format

Return:
1. scope assumptions
2. legal foundation documents
3. product / privacy / commercial docs
4. regulatory-risk triggers
5. founder action list
6. needs-counsel-confirmation

## Common pitfalls

- treating advice as formal legal opinion
- freezing threshold numbers without verification
- ignoring privacy/security operations
- missing fintech, health, children, lending, or brokerage triggers

## Verification checklist

- [ ] I used official-source-first reasoning.
- [ ] I separated confirmed from needs-confirmation items.
- [ ] I flagged regulated-risk features.
- [ ] I gave a founder-usable document map.
