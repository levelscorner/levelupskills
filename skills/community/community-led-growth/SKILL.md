# Community-Led Growth

## Overview
Use this skill to think about community as an intentional company function tied to retention, advocacy, and product momentum.

## Core Principle
Community works when members gain identity, utility, and meaningful participation rather than being treated as passive followers.

## Workflow
1. Clarify audience, product relationship, and desired community role.
2. Diagnose whether the need is acquisition, activation, retention, contribution, or advocacy.
3. Recommend the right community model and loop structure.
4. Define participation mechanics, norms, and stewardship.
5. Distinguish vanity engagement from compounding community value.

## Always cover
- audience and purpose
- community model
- loop design and incentives
- moderation / stewardship needs
- retention and advocacy signals
- failure modes

## Output format
Return:
1. community-growth diagnosis
2. recommended model
3. loop and participation design
4. risks and moderation needs
5. metrics and next experiments
