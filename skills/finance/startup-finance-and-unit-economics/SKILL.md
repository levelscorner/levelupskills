# Startup Finance and Unit Economics

## Overview

Use this skill to translate startup activity into economic reality: how money moves, what actually drives margin, and which decisions improve or damage the business model.

## Core Principle

Finance is not bookkeeping alone. Finance is the discipline that reveals whether growth creates value or only creates more expense.

## Workflow

1. Identify revenue model and cost structure.
2. Separate fixed costs, variable costs, and semi-variable costs.
3. Estimate runway, burn, and cash sensitivity.
4. Evaluate pricing, margin, and payback implications.
5. Recommend operator-level actions, not only accounting observations.

## Always cover

- burn and runway
- gross / contribution margin
- pricing logic
- cost drivers
- reporting cadence
- best / base / worst case scenarios

## Output format

Return:
1. current financial model summary
2. major risks
3. unit-economics view
4. recommended actions
5. metrics to track

## Common pitfalls

- revenue obsession without margin understanding
- ignoring payment, infra, support, and tax costs
- pricing without willingness-to-pay evidence
- no scenario planning

## Verification checklist

- [ ] I separated cash, revenue, and margin issues.
- [ ] I explained the real cost drivers.
- [ ] I gave actionable operator recommendations.
