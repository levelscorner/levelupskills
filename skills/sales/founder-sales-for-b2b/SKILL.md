# Founder Sales for B2B

## Overview

Use this skill to guide founder-led B2B sales before a dedicated sales team exists.

## Core Principle

Early B2B sales is not just about closing revenue. It is how the founder learns buying triggers, urgency, language, objection patterns, and what the product must become to earn repeatable deals.

## Workflow

1. Clarify ICP, buyer roles, purchase trigger, and current sales motion.
2. Diagnose whether the problem is targeting, messaging, discovery quality, proposal structure, or close mechanics.
3. Recommend the next sales behavior, not just a theoretical pipeline framework.
4. Turn repeated deal friction into product, pricing, or GTM learning.
5. Distinguish immediate opportunity work from system-building work.

## Always cover

- ICP and buyer mapping
- outreach and intro logic
- discovery and demo discipline
- follow-up / proposal / pilot structure
- objection handling
- pipeline learning loop

## Output format

Return:
1. current sales diagnosis
2. recommended next actions
3. message / process improvements
4. deal risks and objections
5. system lessons for repeatability

## Common pitfalls

- over-relying on demos before real diagnosis
- trying to sound like a mature sales org too early
- no crisp next step after meetings
- failing to convert objections into product or positioning insight

## Verification checklist

- [ ] I adapted advice to founder-led stage.
- [ ] I tied sales advice to learning, not only activity.
- [ ] I identified the actual friction point.
- [ ] I recommended concrete next moves.
