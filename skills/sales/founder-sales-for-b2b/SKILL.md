# Founder Sales for B2B

## Overview
Use this skill to help a founder sell personally in the early stages: learning the market, qualifying demand, closing initial customers, and building sales judgment before a formal team exists. The goal is not polished sales theater. The goal is direct learning and real revenue.

## When to Use
Use this skill when the main problem is **how the founder should sell personally**.

Typical triggers:
- founder is doing early outbound or inbound selling
- discovery and demos are inconsistent
- early objections are not being learned from well
- proposals or follow-up are weak
- the founder needs to balance selling with product learning
- sales motion exists, but not yet as a formal team function

Do **not** use this as the main skill when:
- the issue is repeatable pipeline mechanics for a non-founder team → use `b2b-sales-discovery-and-pipeline`
- the issue is customer adoption after the deal → use `customer-success-for-b2b`
- the issue is broad GTM experimentation rather than founder-led selling behavior → use `gtm-and-growth-experiments`

## Core Principle
Founder sales works best when each conversation does two jobs at once: generate revenue and improve the founder's understanding of the market, problem, and buying dynamics.

## Always cover
- ideal customer profile
- founder role in outreach and discovery
- objection learning
- demo / proposal / follow-up discipline
- sales-learning feedback loop into product
- handoff or repeatability trigger

## Workflow
1. Clarify product, buyer, and current sales stage.
2. Diagnose whether the bottleneck is outreach, discovery, demo, follow-up, objections, or founder bandwidth.
3. Recommend a founder-sized motion that balances selling and learning.
4. Define the feedback loops from calls into product, messaging, and qualification.
5. State the trigger for when the founder's role should evolve.

## Output format
Return:
1. founder-sales diagnosis
2. recommended motion and call structure
3. objection / demo / follow-up guidance
4. learning loop into product and messaging
5. next actions and transition triggers

## Common pitfalls
1. **Treating founder charisma as a system.** Personal energy does not scale without learning structure.
2. **Selling without capturing what is being learned.** Calls should sharpen the company.
3. **Overbuilding a sales process before signal exists.** Early founder selling should stay light.
4. **Talking product before understanding pain and stakes.** That weakens discovery.
5. **Failing to define when founder-led sales should evolve.** Founder centrality can become a bottleneck later.

## Verification checklist
- [ ] I focused on founder-specific selling realities.
- [ ] I tied sales behavior to both revenue and learning.
- [ ] I covered objections, follow-up, or demo discipline where relevant.
- [ ] I identified triggers for when the motion should become more repeatable or delegated.

## Examples
### Example: early founder selling
Return:
1. The founder needs a lighter, more disciplined motion rather than more hustle.
2. Narrow the ICP and tighten discovery structure.
3. Capture objections and buying blockers systematically after each call.
4. Keep proposals simple and tied to clear next steps.
5. Use deal patterns to decide what product or messaging changes matter.

### Example: founder becoming a bottleneck
Return:
1. The issue is not effort; it is that too much selling knowledge lives in one head.
2. Document the current discovery logic and objection patterns.
3. Define which parts still need founder involvement.
4. Separate learning calls from more repeatable sales motions.
5. Prepare the handoff path before growth makes it urgent.
