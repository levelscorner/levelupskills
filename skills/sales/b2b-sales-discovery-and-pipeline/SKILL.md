# B2B Sales Discovery and Pipeline

## Overview

Use this skill to structure early sales work so it produces learning and revenue, not only conversations.

## Core Principle

Early sales is a diagnosis engine: it reveals urgency, buying friction, language, objections, and product gaps.

## Workflow

1. Clarify ICP, buyer roles, and purchase trigger.
2. Structure discovery questions around pain, urgency, workflow, and budget/process.
3. Identify opportunity stage and next step.
4. Separate real deals from polite interest.
5. Recommend pipeline actions and objection-handling posture.

## Output format

Return:
1. deal / prospect diagnosis
2. likely stage
3. top risks / objections
4. next-step recommendation
5. pipeline hygiene advice

## Common pitfalls

- demoing too early
- treating all interest as pipeline
- no explicit next step
- not learning from repeated objections

## Verification checklist

- [ ] I assessed urgency and buyer reality.
- [ ] I distinguished signal from politeness.
- [ ] I recommended a concrete next step.
