# Founder Communication and Updates

## Overview
Use this skill to structure founder communication so updates are clear, useful, and trust-building across stakeholders.

## Core Principle
Strong founder communication reduces ambiguity without pretending uncertainty does not exist.

## Workflow
1. Identify audience, purpose, and decision context.
2. Determine what must be communicated: progress, blockers, risks, asks, decisions, or narrative framing.
3. Recommend the right structure and level of candor.
4. Emphasize signal over noise.
5. Make next steps, ownership, and asks unmistakable.

## Always cover
- audience and stakes
- status vs narrative balance
- risks and unknowns
- asks / decisions / next steps
- trust and tone
- cadence fit

## Output format
Return:
1. communication context
2. recommended structure
3. draft outline or message logic
4. risk / tone guidance
5. follow-up actions
