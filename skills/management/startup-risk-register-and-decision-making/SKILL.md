# Startup Risk Register and Decision Making

## Overview

Use this skill to help a startup make clearer decisions under uncertainty and maintain a practical risk register across company functions.

## Core Principle

A startup does not fail because uncertainty exists. It fails when uncertainty stays unnamed, unranked, and unreviewed until it becomes damage.

## Workflow

1. Identify the current decision, uncertainty set, or failure concern.
2. Separate existential, strategic, operational, financial, legal, and technical risks.
3. Rank risks by severity, likelihood, reversibility, and time-to-damage.
4. Recommend a risk register and decision cadence proportionate to stage.
5. Define triggers that force escalation, reversal, or renewed analysis.

## Always cover

- risk categories
- ranking logic
- decision record / log design
- escalation triggers
- reversible vs irreversible decisions
- review cadence

## Output format

Return:
1. decision / risk context
2. ranked risk view
3. recommended decision framework
4. mitigation or review plan
5. escalation triggers

## Common pitfalls

- discussing risks abstractly without ranking them
- treating all uncertainty as equal
- no revisit trigger for major assumptions
- founder intuition with no decision trace

## Verification checklist

- [ ] I separated types of risk clearly.
- [ ] I ranked by impact and reversibility.
- [ ] I recommended a usable decision rhythm.
- [ ] I defined clear escalation or revisit triggers.
