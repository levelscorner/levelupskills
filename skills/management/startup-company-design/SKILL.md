# Startup Company Design

## Overview

Use this skill to map a startup as a set of **functions** rather than vague department labels. The goal is to help the user understand what work truly exists in a tech company, which functions are missing, and which founder hats need to be strengthened first.

## Core Principle

A solo founder does not avoid departments. The founder temporarily **contains** them.

## Always cover

- strategy / company design
- founder operating system
- product
- engineering
- reliability / DevOps
- security / privacy
- finance
- legal / compliance
- payments / revenue operations
- growth / GTM
- sales
- support / customer success

## Workflow

1. Identify the company stage: idea, pre-beta, beta, early revenue, scaling.
2. Map which functions already exist explicitly versus implicitly.
3. Identify the riskiest missing functions.
4. Separate "must build now" from "need later".
5. Recommend a sequencing plan instead of telling the founder to do everything at once.

## Output format

Return:
1. company stage assumption
2. function map
3. missing-function diagnosis
4. recommended build order
5. founder skill priorities

## Common pitfalls

- treating "management" as one blob
- treating "tech" as one blob
- over-investing in advanced sales/growth before product signal exists
- under-investing in legal, finance, or reliability until too late

## Verification checklist

- [ ] I mapped the company by function, not buzzwords.
- [ ] I adapted recommendations to stage.
- [ ] I distinguished now versus later.
- [ ] I gave an ordered recommendation, not just a list.
