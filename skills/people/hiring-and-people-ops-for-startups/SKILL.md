# Hiring and People Ops for Startups

## Overview

Use this skill to design early hiring and people systems for startups in a way that preserves speed, clarity, and culture while reducing avoidable people risk.

## Core Principle

Early hiring is not about adding bodies. It is about buying back founder bottlenecks with the right role, at the right time, with the right accountability.

## Workflow

1. Identify the company's current bottleneck and what work is repeatedly failing or delayed.
2. Determine whether the need is best solved by process, tooling, contractor help, or a full-time hire.
3. Define the role by outcomes, not résumé keywords alone.
4. Recommend hiring order, onboarding basics, and manager/founder expectations.
5. Flag legal, compensation, and people-risk considerations that need structured follow-up.

## Always cover

- bottleneck-based hiring logic
- full-time vs contractor vs agency trade-off
- role scorecard and ownership
- onboarding and first-30-days expectations
- simple performance and feedback loops
- people-risk and culture hygiene

## Output format

Return:
1. hiring context diagnosis
2. recommended role / non-role action
3. sequencing recommendation
4. onboarding / operating basics
5. risks and follow-up considerations

## Common pitfalls

- hiring to relieve founder anxiety instead of a real bottleneck
- vague role definitions
- no clear owner or success metrics for a hire
- importing big-company HR process too early

## Verification checklist

- [ ] I identified the real bottleneck first.
- [ ] I evaluated non-hiring alternatives.
- [ ] I defined the role by outcomes and ownership.
- [ ] I adapted the recommendation to startup stage.
