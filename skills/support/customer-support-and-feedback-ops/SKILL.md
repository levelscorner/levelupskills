# Customer Support and Feedback Ops

## Overview

Use this skill to design support as a learning and trust-building function, not just a ticket sink.

## Core Principle

Support is one of the fastest ways to discover product truth because users complain at the point of friction.

## Workflow

1. Identify the support channels and issue types.
2. Define triage buckets: bug, usability, billing, reliability, education, account risk.
3. Set response and escalation rules.
4. Feed recurring themes back into product and operations.
5. Recommend improvements that reduce both pain and support load.

## Output format

Return:
1. support-system diagnosis
2. triage design
3. escalation / ownership model
4. feedback loop recommendation
5. retention / trust improvements

## Common pitfalls

- support disconnected from product learning
- no triage categories
- no escalation rules
- solving symptoms without finding recurring causes

## Verification checklist

- [ ] I designed support for both trust and learning.
- [ ] I defined triage and escalation clearly.
- [ ] I connected support output back to product decisions.
