# Brand Positioning and Messaging

## Overview
Use this skill to help shape how a company is understood, remembered, and chosen.

## Core Principle
Good positioning reduces cognitive load for the customer. It makes the right buyer understand what the product is, why it matters, and why it is meaningfully different.

## Workflow
1. Clarify audience, use case, category context, and alternatives.
2. Diagnose whether the issue is weak positioning, muddy messaging, poor differentiation, or inconsistent voice.
3. Recommend a sharper narrative structure.
4. Align messaging with product truth and buyer pain.
5. Distinguish brand strategy from channel-specific copy execution.

## Always cover
- audience and context
- problem framing
- differentiation logic
- value narrative
- proof and credibility
- messaging risks

## Output format
Return:
1. positioning diagnosis
2. recommended narrative / message architecture
3. differentiation and proof points
4. tone / voice guidance
5. next messaging tests
